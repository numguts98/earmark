Support

Answers to the common questions, and how to reach a person if they don't help.

Get in touch

Email earmark-gifts@outlook.com. Earmark is made by one person, so replies can take a couple of days.

Earmark doesn't appear when I tap Share

iPhone hides newly installed apps in the share sheet. Open Earmark once, then in the app you're sharing from tap Share, swipe to the end of the row of apps and tap More. Tap Edit, then the green plus beside Earmark, then Done.

The photo or price didn't come through

Earmark reads the name, photo and price from the shop's page, and not every shop provides them. The idea is always saved; open it to add a price or photo yourself. If you were offline when you saved it, Earmark tries again the next time you open the app.

I saved an idea but can't find it

If you didn't choose who it was for, it is in the People tab under Not filed yet. Tap it to say who it's for.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed: in Earmark go to Settings, and if it says notifications are off, tap to open iPhone Settings and switch them on. Each date has its own reminder time, shown on the person's Dates tab.

Where is the Christmas checklist?

In the Lists tab. It is part of Earmark Plus, along with idea lists, the thank-you tracker, budgets and the widget.

I bought Earmark Plus and it isn't unlocked

Open Settings, tap Earmark Plus, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. The lifetime option is a single payment and has nothing to cancel.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a person or an idea in the app and it is gone, along with its photos. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything.
