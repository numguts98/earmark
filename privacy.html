Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

Your data stays with you

Everything you put into Earmark – people, gift ideas, photos, dates, sizes and addresses – is stored on your iPhone. Earmark has no accounts and no server, and its developer cannot see any of it.

When iCloud sync is available, your data will also be kept in your own private iCloud storage so it reaches your other devices. It stays yours: it is held by Apple under your Apple Account, and the developer still cannot see it.

No tracking

Earmark contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It does not collect any information about you or how you use the app.

When Earmark uses the internet

Only when you save an idea with a link. Earmark then loads that shop page from your phone, and the product photo it points to (which may be held by a different company the shop uses for images), to pick up the name, photo and price. They see an ordinary visit, as they would if you opened the page in Safari. If the phone is offline it tries again later. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Photos and camera

Photos you take or choose for an idea or a person are kept inside Earmark and are not added to your camera roll. The camera is used only when you choose Take photo.

Contacts

If you choose Add from Contacts, Earmark opens Apple’s contact picker. Only the people you tick are passed to Earmark, once: their name, birthday, anniversary, photo and address are copied into the app. Earmark cannot see the rest of your contacts and never asks for permission to read them.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, your iPhone checks your face or passcode. Earmark only learns whether it worked.

Backups and spreadsheets

When you back up or export a spreadsheet, Earmark writes a file and hands it to the share sheet. A backup holds everything, including photos and addresses. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it, so keep it somewhere safe; Earmark keeps no other copy.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification passes through a server.

Purchases

Earmark Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Earmark only learns whether Plus is unlocked.

Deleting your data

Delete a person or an idea in the app and it is gone, along with its photos. Gifts you noted as received from a person stay in Thank-yous under their name until you delete those too. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the date above will change.
