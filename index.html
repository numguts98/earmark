Remember every gift idea

Earmark is an iPhone app that remembers gifts for you: the ideas you hear, the birthdays coming up, sizes, and what you gave last time.

The other person never needs the app

Earmark is yours alone. Nobody is invited, nobody sees it, and there is no account to create.

Save an idea when you hear it

  • Share from a shop. On a product page, tap Share, then Earmark, then the person's face.
  • Quick add. Long-press the app icon, type a few words and tap a face.
  • Photo. Snap something in a shop. The photo stays inside Earmark, not your camera roll.
  • Siri. "Hey Siri, Earmark idea for Mum."

Then it's waiting for you

  • A page for everyone you buy for: ideas, what you gave before, sizes, likes and dates.
  • A reminder ahead of each occasion, naming the ideas you already have.
  • UK Mother's Day and Father's Day on the right dates, Valentine's Day, Christmas and Secret Santa, with optional Diwali, Eid and Hanukkah.

Earmark Plus

Earmark is free for up to 5 people, with unlimited ideas and reminders. Earmark Plus adds unlimited people, the Christmas checklist, budgets, idea lists, a thank-you tracker and a Home Screen widget.

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone. No account, no tracking, no adverts. Read the privacy policy.
